# Decisions and interpretations

These are the places where PLAN.md left something open and the build had to choose. Each one should be confirmed with the business. Items marked ⚠ touch the data model or the stage rules, and PLAN.md asks for explicit sign-off on those.

## Life cycle (§3)
- ⚠ **Import and Mapping.** An imported row that passes dedupe and the 10-digit check becomes a lead in **Mapping**. If it already has a category, a job title and a geography, it moves on to **Validated** automatically through `transitionLead`, so the history records the step. If it lacks any of those, it stays in Mapping and the data analyst completes it.
- ⚠ **Who signs off each gate.** The Enrolled → Qualified gate needs the **Team 2 leader**. Every other forward gate can be passed by any member of the team that owns the stage, as long as they own the lead, lead the team, or have an open task on it (for example, a tele-caller who was allocated the lead). The data analyst is the sign-off for Mapping.
- ⚠ **Not-interested exits.** A lead can leave as NOT_INTERESTED from Validated, Enrolled, Qualified or Active. DROPPED is available from Sourced, Selected and Joined and needs a reason code. Terminal stages cannot be reopened. Reopening would be a rule change and needs sign-off.
- **Rejection and no-show at interview.** Either one sends the lead to DROPPED, unless the lead has another live submission to a different vacancy.
- ⚠ **Team 3 allocates qualified leads to Team 2.** A lead that is enrolled and qualified first waits in the **Team 3 leader's pool** (`/allocation`, visible read-only to recruiters and the Team 2 leader). The Team 3 leader hands it, one category at a time (Doctor, Pharmacy, …) or lead by lead, to a Team 2 sourcer, who becomes the owner. The Qualified → Active gate requires this allocation. Leads that were already Qualified or Active when this shipped were marked allocated to their current owner, so only newly qualified leads wait for Team 3. The pool suggests the sourcer that Admin → Assignment rules picks for the category. The Team 2 member who scrutinised the lead stays the owner until it is allocated, so the "qualified leads" KPI still credits them.
  - **Candidate detail access from the pool is limited to the Team 3 leader.** The pool listing itself (name, category, experience, tier — no contact details) is visible to every Team 3 recruiter, since `/allocation`'s own query doesn't restrict by owner. Opening a candidate's full record (`/leads/:id`, decrypted contact details, resume/video download) from that listing is restricted to the Team 3 leader (and admin): a regular recruiter has no ownership, task or team-stage claim on a Qualified/Active lead, so `leadScope` does not extend to them for it. Two concurrent allocation requests (e.g. the leader double-clicking, or the leader and an admin allocating the same category at once) can no longer both claim the same lead — `allocateQualified` and `allocateColdCalls` now claim each lead with a conditional update and silently skip any that another request already took, rather than the second write overwriting the first.
- **Availability check-ins.** A lead gets its first availability check-in task when the Team 3 leader allocates it to a Team 2 sourcer, and then another every 60 days while it stays Qualified. A cold lead that later confirms availability counts as a cold → warm conversion.
- ⚠ **Engagement tiers (super active / active / warm / cold).** Qualified and Active leads are tiered by days since they were **last engaged**: the latest of enrolment (registering on the NT platform), an NT platform / app visit (`POST /v1/webhooks/nt-activity`), or the candidate confirming they need a job. Defaults: super active ≤ 5 days, active ≤ 14 days ("visited in the previous week"), warm ≤ 60 days, and cold beyond 60 days (more than 2 months). Admins can change the thresholds. The tier is computed from `last_engaged_at` when it is read, so it never goes stale. It is separate from the availability check-in's cold / warm flag (`is_cold`), which is left as it was.
- ⚠ **Cold-lead WhatsApp.** A daily 10:00 IST sweep sends the `reengage_cold_whatsapp` template once to every lead that has gone cold since it was last engaged, up to `reengageDailyLimit` per day (default 200) so the first run does not message the whole backlog at once. Deactivating the template turns the sweep off. A reply within 30 days is read for job intent: a quick-reply button id `JOB_YES` / `JOB_NO`, or a plain "yes" / "haan" / "no". "Yes" makes the lead **super active immediately** and tells the owner. "No" is recorded and the lead stays cold. Anything else opens a "read WhatsApp reply" task for the owner, who can confirm the job need by hand (as they can after any call). Replies that are not answering a re-engagement message (for example "YES" to an interview confirmation) are stored but never change the tier.
- **Day-30 retention.** A passed day-30 check moves the lead to **Successful** and adds one filled opening to the vacancy. When every opening is filled, the vacancy closes.

- ⚠ **Cold-lead calls (Team 2).** Cold leads are also worked by phone, as on the TA team 2 sheet, alongside the automatic WhatsApp. The **Team 2 leader** allocates them from a pool (`/cold-calls`: a whole category, the next N of a category, or ticked leads) to a Team 2 member; the daily sweep reminds the leader how many are waiting. The caller logs **No answer**, **Answered – not looking** or **Needs a job** (→ super active at once). No answer comes back as a recall after `coldCallRecallHours` (24) until `coldCallMaxAttempts` (3). The first call on an allocation is an "attempt" and later ones "re-attempts" (recalls); both are counted separately. Cold calls do not touch Team 1's attempt counter (the Unreachable cap). A lead that re-engages and goes cold again can be allocated again.

## Data (§4)
- ⚠ **NT vs non-NT.** Non-NT means the job-portal sources: NAUKRI, LINKEDIN, INDEED and a new OTHER_PORTAL value. Every other source is part of Nextenti's own funnel and counts as NT. `is_nt_source` is derived from the source.
- ⚠ **Fields added to the schema** so that KPIs can be calculated from events:
  - `lead_stage_history.prev_owner_user_id` and `owner_user_id`: ownership snapshots, used to credit the right agent.
  - `candidates.scrutinized_at` and `scrutinized_by_id`
  - `candidates.created_by_id`
  - `availability_checks.was_cold`
  - `vacancies.sourcer_id` and `was_pending`
  - `missed_calls`: a new table for the Team 1b inbox
  - `scheduled_jobs`: a new table that stores reminders durably
- **Candidate codes** are generated from a sequence, in the form NTC000001. Vacancy codes follow the pattern VAC00001.
- **Contact details** (mobile, alternate mobile, email) are stored AES-GCM encrypted, and each has an HMAC blind index. Search by the last 4 digits uses `mobile_last4`.

## KPIs (§6)
- **"Opening vacancies"** means vacancies that were open at the start of the period. The Team 3 rates (interviews, offers, joinings and closures) divide by *vacancies in hand*, which is opening + newly added, so that a week that starts with nothing open does not divide by zero.
- **"Pending"** vacancies are OPEN vacancies from an earlier day (IST) that are still below the CV target. A nightly job marks them. "Closed pending" counts pending vacancies whose sourcing completed during the period.
- **"Enrolled screened by TL" and "approved after scrutiny"** (Team 1a) are cohort counts. They look at the leads a TA lead enrolled in the period and count how many Team 2 has since scrutinised or qualified.
- **"Mismatched qualified leads"** (Team 4) means leads qualified in the period whose CV a recruiter later rejected.
- **Working days** exclude Sundays and entries in the holiday calendar. Saturday counts as a working day, and the same calendar applies to the 1-working-day red-flag SLA.
- **Excel layout.** The export follows the layout implied by PLAN §6: one tab per team, KPIs as rows, agents as columns, then team total and target, followed by the "Red flags noticed" and "Action taken" rows. It should be compared against the real "Weekly and monthly Analysis" sheet, and cell positions adjusted in `src/kpi/export.ts` if needed.

## Daily dashboard — the TA team 1 / team 2 monthly workbooks
The workbooks the team filled in by hand (`TA team 1 monthly dash board.xlsx`, `TA team 2 monthly dash board.xlsx`) are rebuilt from CRM events at `/kpi/daily`, with the same columns, a row per day, Monday–Sunday weeks clipped to the month (Week 4 of September = 21–27), a monthly total and the KPI rows. **Download Excel** gives the same layout: a tab per person plus Consolidated. Every day's numbers are registry KPIs (`src/kpi/definitions.ts`), so they agree with the KPI sheets; weeks and months are the sums of the days and the KPI rows divide those sums, as the workbook formulas do. How each column is read:
- ⚠ **Team 1 · allocated validated leads.** *Allocated* = NT-funnel leads entering Validated owned by the TA lead. *Attempted / answered / interested* = the lead's **first** contact (answered = any outcome but Unanswered; interested = link sent or enrolled). *Enrolled from the allocated leads* = enrolments that took at most one attempt; *enrolled from re-attempted* = more than one; *follow-ups* = re-attempts on leads left interested (Bb). *Scrutinised / approved by the TL* = of that day's NT enrolments, how many Team 2 has scrutinised / qualified.
- ⚠ **Team 1 · allocated job postings.** Vacancies now carry a **TA lead**, given the posting by category through Admin → Assignment rules (Team 1a), as the sourcer is for Team 2; only new vacancies get one. TA leads can read vacancies (not create them). *CVs downloaded* = portal leads the TA lead entered: LinkedIn vs Naukri / Indeed / other portals. *Scrutinised / approved* = of that day's portal (non-NT) enrolments.
- ⚠ **Team 2 · allocated job postings.** *Job postings* = vacancies with the agent as sourcer. *Matching CVs from NT / non-NT* = candidates the agent invited to apply or submitted that day, by funnel; *CVs given to Team 3* = CVs submitted to recruiters. *Scrutinised / approved by the TL* = enrolment scrutiny and Enrolled → Qualified, as on the KPI sheet.
- **Team 2 · allocated cold leads** is the cold-lead call funnel above.
- **Vacancies** have free-text *Description* and *Mandatory attributes* fields (shown in the job-post columns).
- **Red flags** appear once per sheet (the workbooks repeat the columns in each block; a CRM red flag is not tied to a block): a person's tab shows the flags raised against them, Consolidated shows all the team's flags, with the CAPA suggested, open / closed and the TAT.
- **Workbook formula fixes.** "% of the total enrolled leads over answered leads" is total enrolled / answered, as its label says (the workbook added two percentages); the workbook's "Total enrolled CVs" KPI (`#VALUE!`) is left out; Consolidated sums every member (the workbook's Consolidated tab read Mounika's rows one day off). The free-text *Remarks* column is not carried over.
